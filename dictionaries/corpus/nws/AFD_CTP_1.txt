.DISCUSSION... KEY MESSAGE 1: Seasonably cooler this weekend into next week with bulk of precipitation confined near and southeast of Lake Erie

Following one of the warmest starts to November on record in central PA, (see climate section) we'll make a trip on the weather coaster back to seasonably cooler temps this weekend through the upcoming work week (though a slight rebound in temps will occur Sunday through Tuesday).

A compact and seasonably strong closed upper low near Buffalo, NY this afternoon will move across southern New York and lift northeast away from the area by Sunday morning. This feature accompanied by cool air aloft and mean west to NW flow will bring scattered to numerous showers to start the first full weekend of November.

The rest of today into early next week will be noticeably cooler and feel rather chilly given how warm it has been during the day over the past week or so. Breezy conditions will help to bring the chill factor. Max Wind gusts will top out in the mid 25-35mph range and could peak around 40 mph on Monday.

There is a growing model signal for a more substantial long wave trough to carve out over the eastern U.S. by the end of the week. While still very preliminary given the extended forecast length and associated uncertainly/low confidence, it appears the coldest air of the Fall season will attempt to expand southward late next week with the upper trough and could be a factor with a potential coastal storm supplying a swath of precipitation including accumulating snow particularly in the higher elevations.
