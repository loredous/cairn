.DISCUSSION... Issued at 138 PM EST Sat Nov 7 2026

- Clearing tonight, dry through Wednesday

The upper low that created showers yesterday is exiting the state to the east. Visible satellite loop shows a well defined back edge to the clouds and clearing has reached the lake shore. As the upper low moves east and ridging builds in from the west, the clearing will move across the rest of the cwa this afternoon/evening.

The upper flow pattern across the northern tier of states is generally northwest to southeast. Typical of this time of year, short waves moving through the flow will cross the state frequently. The next wave will move through Sunday followed by another wave over northern Lower Tuesday. A much more robust short wave will dig south across the Great Lakes Wednesday night and Thursday. The wave that moves through Sunday has little moisture with it and we won't see anything more than clouds late tonight and Sunday morning.

The short wave Tuesday moves across Lake Superior and won't produce much more than some clouds Tuesday night and Wednesday.

The deeper wave Wednesday/Thursday may give us some precipitation, depending on where the surface low ends up. Models are showing the surface low moving across the northern OH Valley Wednesday/Thursday and would pull the precipitation south. .AVIATION /06Z TAFS THROUGH 06Z MONDAY/... Issued at 1239 AM EST Sun Nov 8 2026

We are starting out the forecast period with mainly clear skies across all of the terminals. A weak front over Wisconsin is poised to move through the area today. This will bring a period of a few hours of low clouds based in the lower MVFR category from the NW beginning this morning, and exiting the area to the SE this afternoon. As this front moves through, we will see winds increase from the WSW with gusts of 25 to 30 knots likely.

We will see clearing then take place this afternoon for a bit. Then additional lower clouds will drop in just after sunset from North to South. The northern terminals will see more extensive cloud cover. This cloud cover is expected to be VFR, so it will not be as much of an impact as the frontal clouds.
