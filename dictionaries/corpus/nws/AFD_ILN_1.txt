.DISCUSSION... KEY MESSAGE 1)

Mid and upper level low over the Eastern Great Lakes to track east into New England overnight. Low clouds over the area will erode from west to east late in the day thru this evening. In the wake of the low a northwesterly flow aloft will develop with surface high pressure over the Mid MS Valley building into the TN Valley overnight. Clearing skies and light winds will result in chilly overnight low temperatures. Expect lows to range from the upper 20s to the lower 30s. Have continued the freeze warning for tonight and Sunday morning for counties where the frost/freeze season continues.

KEY MESSAGE 2)

Northwest flow aloft will continue Sunday and then back more westerly early next week. As surface high pressure builds to our southeast a a southwest low level flow will develop. As a result, a dry forecast is expected through mid week with highs moderating from the 50s on Sunday and Monday into the lower and middle 60s Tuesday.

A digging system will result in the passage of a surface cold front late Wednesday into Thursday. This system leads to a mid level low that develops over the Great Lakes late in the week. This will lead to a chance of rain beginning Wednesday night and continuing into Thursday. The mid level low will keep the threat for precipitation in the forecast into Friday. Temperatures will turn colder with highs in the 50s Thursday, the 40s for Friday, and the upper 40s to around 50 Saturday.
