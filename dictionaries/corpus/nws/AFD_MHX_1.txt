.DISCUSSION... KEY MESSAGE 1...Seasonably dry and temperature airmass (perhaps a couple degrees below normal) is being ushered in behind this morning's cold front as it pushes further offshore. In the near term, low stratus will continue dissipating, leading to clear skies this evening. The high will become the dominant weather feature for the weekend and much of next week. Cooler temperatures with highs in the 60s prevail today and tomorrow before a slight warm-up next week into the low 70s, although humidity will remain low. We will have to watch for the risk of some frost over the upcoming nights, although chances have decreased for tonight and Sunday night, and increased for Monday night. Tonight a mixture of high clouds and light winds should keep low temps around 40 inland. NBM had low temps in the mid to upper 30s, but this seems to be an erroneous bias correction as all other available guidance is not that low, and high clouds/light winds should prevent proper radiational cooling. Tomorrow night skies are clear, but light winds are still expected to continue, once again lessening radiational cooling. Monday night however, we have the high firmly overhead with clear skies and calm winds, paired with dewpoints in the 30s. This results in the best chance for frost concerns, and will have to be monitored in subsequent updates. Manually brought the temps down by blending in NBM/NBM10 for the typical radiational cooling areas in Carteret, Onslow, Jones, and Duplin counties, with minTs in the mid 30s Tuesday morning in those locations.

Beyond mid-week most guidance depicts an increasingly unsettled pattern as complex mid-level low deepens over the eastern CONUS. There is at least some signal for a developing low off our coast beyond Wednesday, although its evolution and timing remain highly uncertain.

Guidance is pretty locked in on unseasonable cold temperatures next weekend behind the low pressure system. While there is inherent uncertainty 7 days out, CPC does have a slight risk of much below normal temperatures for our coastal plain counties next weekend. This will be worth monitoring for any freeze headlines, as the end of the growing season is Nov 25 for our land forecast zones.
