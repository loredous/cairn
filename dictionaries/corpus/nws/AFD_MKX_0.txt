.LONG TERM... Issued 210 PM CST Sat Nov 7 2026

Monday through Saturday:

High pressure gradually sinks builds across the Lower Mississippi Valley on Monday, turning winds southwesterly and bringing a return to highs around 60 degrees. A strong 500 mb shortwave moving through central Ontario will allow for winds to remain gusty into the evening hours before diminishing. A weak cold front will follow, bringing northwest winds on Tuesday. Still anticipating temperatures around 60 degrees Tuesday afternoon before reinforcing cold high pressure builds in Tuesday evening.

Wednesday, another shortwave propagating through the mean flow aloft looks to move southeastward through the Canadian Prairies/northern Plains and bring a few chances for rain showers (15-20%) as well as highs in the low 50s with plenty of cloud cover. With this less intense shortwave, model uncertainty with placement remains. Euro is the outlier at this time, showing a phasing, deepening system across Illinois as the shortwave swings farther south and interacts with warm, moist air from the Gulf, while GFS/Canadian indicate a weaker system blending into the larger semi-stationary low over the Canadian Maritimes. Kept NBM PoPs for this run due to this uncertainty, but confidence in some kind of scattered showers is increasing.

Moving into Thursday, models have converged on a period of north to northeast flow, bringing large fetch and lake effect showers from Lake Michigan throughout the day through the overnight hours and perhaps as late as Friday night before winds shift north to northwest and bring an end to precipitation. With Lake Michigan water temperatures still in the 50s and inland temperatures falling into the mid to low 30s inland, a mix of rain and snow is possible. At this time, not anticipating a transition to pure snow, but colder trends will be monitored. Duration of lake effect precipitation and cold air potential will be dependent on the depth and placement of a developing Nor'easter along the Atlantic coastal states, with a stronger, farther north solution being the best for longer duration fetch through Friday night. Outside of lake effect regions, expect dry and cloudy conditions Thursday and Friday with highs in the mid-40s and lows around 30 degrees.

High pressure returns for Saturday, with northwest winds and plenty of sunshine. Expect highs around 50 degrees.
