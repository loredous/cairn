.DISCUSSION... .KEY MESSAGE 1... Strong upper level low continues to move towards the Canadian Maritimes with its associated surface low retrograding back northwest within Southeast Canada. In the mid levels, the region will be in between shortwaves, with some ridging taking place. Surface high pressure remains well south and west as the low strengthens as it moves farther northeast from the local area.

Winds will remain elevated as the pressure gradient persists with low temperatures tonight in the upper 30s to upper 40s.

For Sunday, temperatures will mainly be in the upper 50s to lower 60s. Expect gusty winds to persist through Monday as the pressure gradient persisting between low pressure over eastern Canada and high pressure to our south and west.

Lows Sunday night will be in the upper 30s to near 50 with Monday's high in the upper 50s to low 60s.

.KEY MESSAGE 2... Mid levels transition from WNW to ESE type flow to more SW to NE towards the end of the week. At the surface, a few frontal systems pass within the early to midweek period, but they are weak in magnitude. Dry conditions are forecast to prevail.

Towards the end of next week, more of a low pressure influence develops with more meridional flow aloft. Models are indicating both the potential of larger low pressure area as well as a stronger low pressure area. Forecast has more chances of rain Thursday through Friday as a result. Too early to determine assessment for any impacts with model run to run variability with regard to the position of the low and orientation of upper level trough.
