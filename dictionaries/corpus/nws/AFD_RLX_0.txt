.DISCUSSION... KEY MESSAGE 1...

Freezing conditions are expected across much of the CWA this morning, as sky cover gradually clears, and winds remain light to calm, as high pressure nudges into the area from the west. This will set up a period of dry conditions through at least early next week, along with a gradual warming trend, particularly by Tuesday as southerly flow increases out ahead of an approaching system.

KEY MESSAGE 2...

By Wednesday into Thursday, a potent upper level trough and associated cold front will approach the area. Precipitation will start out as rain across the area, and could be moderate in intensity at times. Precipitation will gradually transition over to snow, or a rain snow mix at times, as a deep upper trough forms across the eastern U.S., ushering in much colder and blustery conditions. At least minor accumulations are possible across the mountains, as a prolonged period of northwesterly upslope flow takes hold.
