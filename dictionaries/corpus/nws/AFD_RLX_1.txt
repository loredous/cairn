.DISCUSSION... KEY MESSAGE 1...

High pressure continues to nudge in keeping our weather dry, albeit cloudy today, especially across the mountains where low clouds will linger through Sunday morning. High temperatures will be below normal today with the lowlands staying in the 40s to low 50s. The mountains will remain in the 30s and 40s. A warming trend starts tomorrow with 50s and 60s arriving across the area. This warming trend will continue through at least mid week.

Overnight lows will be quite chilly and below normal the next few nights, especially tonight into Sunday morning. Lows tonight are projected to drop into the mid 20s to the mid 30s. Hard freeze and widespread frost is anticipated Sunday morning and again Monday morning, possibly even Tuesday morning due to these temperatures. As such, a freeze warning is in effect across our active growing season counties from midnight tonight until 10 AM Sunday morning. Additional headlines are possible for Monday morning.

KEY MESSAGE 2...

Dry weather is expected to last through at least Wednesday. A cold front moves into the area and stalls Wednesday night into Thursday. A large, amplified, upper-level disturbance moves through on Friday after small shortwave disturbances cross. These features are projected to bring active weather Thursday through Saturday. Mostly all rain is expected throughout the event, but some rain/snow mix maybe possible at times across the mountains Thursday night through Friday as colder temperatures move in. Models still diverge on a solution this far out so confidence is low.

Strong, blustery northwesterly flow and subsequent colder air will move into the area Friday and Saturday with temperatures projected to be much below normal for next weekend.
