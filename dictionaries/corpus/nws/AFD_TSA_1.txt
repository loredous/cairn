.SHORT TERM... (Through tonight ) Issued at 1230 PM CST Sat Nov 7 2026

Sfc high is forecast to settle across the forecast area tonight, however cooling potential will be somewhat mitigated by increasing high clouds across the south and air that is not particularly dry. Forecast will side toward a cooler scenario with patchy fog possible. Areas of frost are likely across northern locations and colder valleys may see a brief light freeze.

&&

.LONG TERM... (Sunday through Saturday) Issued at 1230 PM CST Sat Nov 7 2026

Warming trend begins Sunday with above normal temperatures returning and persisting through mid next week. The upper low currently across far SW TX will weaken as it slowly evolves northeastward and the majority of guidance keep any rains well south of the local area. A fast moving wave will move across the region Tuesday night or Wednesday with a low chance of light rain.

Stronger ridging aloft is forecast to develop across the western CONUS placing dry and cooler northwesterly flow aloft downstream. This will act to reinforce the airmass change behind the Wednesday wave and bring cooler temperatures, nearer to seasonal normals, for late week into next weekend. Dry weather also appears likely to continue into next weekend.
