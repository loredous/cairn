BULLETIN - EAS ACTIVATION REQUESTED Flash Flood Warning National Weather Service Tucson AZ 455 PM MST Sun Nov 1 2026

The National Weather Service in Tucson has extended the

* Flash Flood Warning for... Eastern Graham County in southeastern Arizona... Greenlee County in southeastern Arizona...

* Until 630 PM MST.

* At 455 PM MST, Doppler radar and automated rain gauges indicated 1 to 2 inches of rain had fallen across eastern Graham and Greenlee county earlier today, with light to moderate showers persisting across the area. Rainfall runoff on already saturated soils will result in swift water runoff... resulting in a persisting threat for flash flooding.

HAZARD...Flash flooding caused by heavy rain.

SOURCE...Radar and automated gauges.

IMPACT...Flash flooding of small creeks and streams, urban areas, highways, streets and underpasses as well as other poor drainage and low-lying areas.
