BULLETIN - EAS ACTIVATION REQUESTED Flash Flood Warning National Weather Service Tucson AZ 453 PM MST Sun Nov 1 2026

The National Weather Service in Tucson has extended the

* Flash Flood Warning for... Western Cochise County in southeastern Arizona... Southwestern Graham County in southeastern Arizona... East Central Pima County in southeastern Arizona... Eastern Santa Cruz County in southeastern Arizona...

* Until 630 PM MST.

* At 453 PM MST, Doppler radar and automated rain gauges indicated 1 to 2 inches of rain had fallen across western Cochise and southwest Graham county today. Rainfall runoff on already saturated soils will continue to result in flash flooding.

HAZARD...Flash flooding caused by heavy rain.

SOURCE...Radar and automated gauges.

IMPACT...Flash flooding of small creeks and streams, urban areas, highways, streets and underpasses as well as other poor drainage and low-lying areas.

* Some locations that will experience flash flooding include... Sierra Vista, Safford, Benson, Willcox, Hereford, Thatcher, Whetstone, Huachuca City, St. David, Tombstone, Sonoita, Dragoon, Elgin, Pearce-Sunsites, Kartchner Caverns State Park, Roper Lake State Park, Fort Huachuca, Parker Canyon Lake, Palominas and Pima.

This includes the following Flash Flood Prone Locations... Moson Rd from HWY 90 and Hereford Rd.

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

Turn around, don't drown when encountering flooded roads. Most flood deaths occur in vehicles.

Be aware of your surroundings and do not drive on flooded roads.
