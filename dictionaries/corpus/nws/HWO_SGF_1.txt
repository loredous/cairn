This Hazardous Weather Outlook is for portions of the Missouri Ozarks and extreme southeast Kansas.

.DAY ONE...Today and Tonight.

Areas of frost and freeze are possible this morning, however persistent cloud cover leads to low confidence in this occuring.

.DAYS TWO THROUGH SEVEN...Sunday through Friday.

There is a higher chance of frost and freeze Sunday morning. The coldest temperatures are expected east of Highway 65, with lows in the low to mid 30s across much of the area.
