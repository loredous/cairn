Survey Summary: A tornado developed south of I-25 with more impactful damage starting in the Via Caballero neighborhood in the south central portion of Santa Fe just north of I-25. The tornado traveled northward for approximately 2.25 miles, terminating near the College of Santa Fe. Much of the damage was along Rancho Siringo and Camino Chueco Roads. Damage included broken power poles, uprooted or damaged trees, damage to roofs. Ten homes sustained damaged with one home having the majority of the roof removed and a collapsed wall. This tornado has been given a rating of EF- 1 with peak winds of 110 mph.
