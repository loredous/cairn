Public Information Statement National Weather Service Amarillo TX 848 AM CST Mon Nov 2 2026

...Preliminary Storm Survey Scheduled for Today for the Area Near Keyes in Cimarron County Oklahoma...

The National Weather Service office in Amarillo TX will conduct a storm survey around Today for the area near Keyes in Cimarron County Oklahoma. The survey is in relation to the severe thunderstorms that moved through the area on November 1 2026.

A final assessment including results of the survey are expected to be completed and transmitted via a Public Information Statement by later today.

The storm survey information will also be available on our website at http://www.weather.gov/ama
