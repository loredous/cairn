Public Information Statement National Weather Service Wilmington OH 732 AM EST Sun Nov 1 2026

...GROWING SEASON HAS ENDED FOR PORTIONS OF CENTRAL/WESTERN OHIO AND SOUTHEAST INDIANA...

As of November 1st, the National Weather Service office in Wilmington, OH will no longer issue Frost/Freeze headlines for the following counties:

In Ohio...

Mercer, Auglaize, Hardin, Darke, Shelby, Logan, Union, Delaware, Licking, Miami, Champaign, Clark, Madison, Fairfield, Preble, Montgomery, Greene, Fayette, Hocking, Butler, Warren, Clinton.

In Indiana...

Wayne, Fayette, Union, Franklin.

All other counties remain in an active growing season, with Frost/Freeze headlines continuing as needed until November 7th unless a hard freeze occurs before then.

A map of the counties that remain in the program can be found at www.weather.gov/iln/fallfrostfreeze.
