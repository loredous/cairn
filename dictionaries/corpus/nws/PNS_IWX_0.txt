...End of the Climatological Growing Season...

The growing season has ended for northern Indiana and northwest Ohio. As a result, frost and freeze headlines will be suspended until Spring 2027.

The growing season ended in far southern Lower Michigan on October 18 after instances of frost or freeze conditions were reported.
