Public Information Statement National Weather Service New Orleans LA 930 AM CST Wed Nov 4 2026

...NEW ORLEANS NOAA WEATHER RADIO OUTAGE...

The New Orleans NOAA Weather Radio, broadcasting on a frequency of 162.550 MHz, will be off the air between 10 am and 4 pm today.

Please refer to local media and commercial radio for the latest weather information. The following nearby NOAA Weather radio transmitters that can be used include: Morgan City on a frequency of 162.475 MHz, Bogalusa on a frequency of 162.525 MHz, and Buras on a frequency of 162.475 MHz.

The latest weather information is also available at our website, at www.weather.gov/neworleans

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.
