...MEDFORD OREGON WEATHER REVIEW: October 2026...

October 2026 was warmer and drier than normal.

Summer continued on through the first 10 days of October, with continued high pressure keeping conditions dry and temperatures between 5 and 15 degrees above normal. A dry cold front passed through the area on the 10th, ushering in several days of cooler than normal temperatures through the 13th. A solid freeze occured on the morning of the 12th, ending the local growing season. There was a return of mostly warmer than normal temperatures from the 14th though the 21st, although a passing weak front did produce a day of near normal temperatures and a trace of rain on the 19th. Autumn finally arrived in full force for the rest of the month, with continued below normal temperatures and several days of rain.

The average temperature for the month was 57.5 degrees, which is 1.4 degrees above normal. The average maximum temperature was 73.2 degrees, which is 3.1 degrees above normal. The average minimum temperature was 41.7 degrees, which is 0.4 degrees below normal. The highest temperature for the month was 94 degrees which occurred on the 7th. The lowest temperature for the month was 28 degrees which occurred on the 24th.

There were 25 clear days, 6 partly cloudy days, and zero cloudy days.

Total precipitation for the month was 0.65 inches which is 0.57 inches below normal. Total precipitation for the water year (beginning October 1st) is 0.65 inches which is 0.57 inches below normal. Calendar year precipitation is 8.69 inches which is 3.6 inches below normal.

Total snowfall for the month was 0.0 inches which is normal. Total snowfall for the season (beginning July 1st) is 0.0 inches which is normal.

The average wind speed for the month was 2.4 mph. The fastest recorded two-minute wind speed was 23 mph from the NW which occurred on the 11th. The peak wind was 34 mph from the NW which occurred on the 11th.

The highest pressure for the month was 30.32 inches on the 15th. The lowest pressure for the month was 29.69 inches on the 9th.

A daily record high temperature of 88 degrees was set on the 16th, tying the previous record from 2022.
