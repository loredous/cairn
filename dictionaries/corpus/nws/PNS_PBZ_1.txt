PUBLIC INFORMATION STATEMENT National Weather Service Pittsburgh PA 717 AM EST Sun Nov 1 2026

...Growing Season Ended For Western PA, Eastern OH, and Northern WV...

In collaboration with partners and neighboring offices, the National Weather Service Pittsburgh office has ended the growing season for its area of responsibility. This covers portions of eastern Ohio, western Pennsylvania, and northern West Virginia.

The growing season will return in Spring 2027, at which point Frost and Freeze headlines may be issued again.
