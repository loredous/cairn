Public Information Statement National Weather Service Charleston WV 307 AM EST Mon Nov 2 2026

...GROWING SEASON HAS ENDED FOR MUCH OF SOUTHEAST OHIO AND NORTHERN WEST VIRGINIA...

As of November 1st, 2026, the National Weather Service Charleston, WV forecast office will no longer issue Frost and Freeze Watches, Warnings and Advisories for the 2026 growing season for northern West Virginia and southeast Ohio, with the exception of Lawrence County Ohio. This is due to being well past the normal end of the growing season.

With the exception of Raleigh County West Virginia, areas along and south of I-64 including Lawrence County Ohio will remain in an active growing season and will continue to see future headlines for Frost/Freeze conditions.
