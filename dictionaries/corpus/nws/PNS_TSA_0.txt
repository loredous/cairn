...NWS Damage Survey for 11/02-03/2026 Tornado Event - Update 1...

.Update...Updated to include the Cedarville AR tornado

.TALALA OKLAHOMA TORNADO...

Rating: EF-0 Estimated Peak Wind: 80 to 85 mph Path Length /statute/: 2.7 miles Path Width /maximum/: 275 yards Fatalities: 0 Injuries: 0

Start Date: 11/02/2026 Start Time: 11:16 PM CST Start Location: 4 NW Oologah / Rogers County / OK Start Lat/Lon: 36.484 / -95.764

End Date: 11/02/2026 End Time: 11:20 PM CST End Location: 2 WSW Talala / Rogers County / OK End Lat/Lon: 36.516 / -95.735

Survey Summary: This tornado developed south of Big Sky Lane and east of the S 4060 Road. It moved northeast, crossing Big Sky Lane and the E 360 Road, snapping numerous large tree limbs before moving into open terrain. The tornado dissipated south of the E 340 Road. It produced a tornadic debris signature from the KINX WSR-88D radar, and was likely more intense north of the E 360 Road (based on radar data), but any damage in that part of the path was inaccessible by road, and therefore, couldn't be assessed.

.ADAIR OKLAHOMA TORNADO...

Rating: EF-1 Estimated Peak Wind: 95 to 105 mph Path Length /statute/: 3.1 miles Path Width /maximum/: 500 yards Fatalities: 0 Injuries: 0

Start Date: 11/03/2026 Start Time: 01:10 AM CST Start Location: 1 NE Adair / Mayes County / OK Start Lat/Lon: 36.449 / -95.248

End Date: 11/03/2026 End Time: 01:14 AM CST End Location: 3 SSW Big Cabin / Mayes County / OK End Lat/Lon: 36.494 / -95.236

Survey Summary: This tornado developed south of the E 390 Road and east of the N 435 Road. It moved north-northeast across the E 390 Road, where numerous trees were uprooted, and a home and outbuilding were damaged. The tornado then moved through open terrain that was inaccessible by road for about a mile and a half. It then snapped several power poles south of the E 370 Road, uprooted trees along the E 370 Road and S 4360 Road, and dissipated south of the E 360 Road. The tornado produced a tornadic debris signature from the KINX WSR-88D.

.CEDARVILLE AR TORNADO...

Rating: EF-1 Estimated Peak Wind: 90 to 100 mph Path Length /statute/: 2.9 miles Path Width /maximum/: 175 yards Fatalities: 0 Injuries: 0

Start Date: 11/03/2026 Start Time: 02:15 AM CST Start Location: 2 W Cedarville / Crawford County / AR Start Lat/Lon: 35.572 / -94.400

End Date: 11/03/2026 End Time: 02:20 AM CST End Location: 3 SSE Natural Dam / Crawford County / AR End Lat/Lon: 35.610 / -94.378

Survey Summary: This tornado developed over rugged terrain west of Cedarville on a ridgetop. It moved north-northeast across Rocky Road and Shiloh Road, where trees were uprooted and a few were snapped, and numerous large tree limbs were snapped. The tornado then moved northeast through rugged terrain after crossing Broken Trail, and then uprooted a few trees and snapped large tree limbs along Creekside Way and finally on Highway 59 before dissipating. The tornado produced a tornadic debris signature from the KSRX WSR- 88D.
