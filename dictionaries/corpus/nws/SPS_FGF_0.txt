...AREAS OF FOG ACROSS PORTIONS OF NORTHEAST ND...

Localized areas of fog reducing visibility to around one-quarter mile have developed across portions of northeast ND, especially near the Pembina Escarpment. Travelers should be prepared for rapidly deteriorating visibility, and to use extra caution at intersections, railroad crossings, and school bus stops. The fog is expected to start improving early this afternoon.
