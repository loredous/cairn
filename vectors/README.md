## `ldpc_<code>.json` (header, control, data)

One file per code. Top-level fields: `format` (`cairn-ldpc-vectors`),
`version`, `license`, `code`, `base_graph`, `lifting` (Z), `message_bits`,
`info_bits` (message plus CRC-16), `codeword_bits` (N before puncturing, as in
`codes/*.alist`), `coded_lengths`, and `vectors`.

Each vector has:

- `name`: `zeros`, `ones`, `counting` (byte i is i mod 256) or `randomN`
  (N = 1, 2, 3: bytes of SHA-256 of the ASCII string
  `cairn-ldpc-vector:N:counter`, counter = 0, 1, ... concatenated).
- `message_hex`: the message bytes (128, 240 or 1008 bits).
- `info_hex`: message followed by its CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE (poly 0x1021, init
  0xFFFF, no reflection, no final XOR; computed over the message bits, sent
  most significant bit first, big-endian). This is the code's information
  bits (144, 256 or 1024).
- `codeword_hex`: the full systematic codeword of N bits: information bits,
  then K - K' zero filler bits, then parity. Includes the punctured bits.
- `rate_matched_hex`: map from on-air length E to the E bits sent, i.e. the
  codeword bits selected by `codes/<stem>_ratematch.txt`, in order.

Bit order: every `*_hex` field packs bits big-endian. The first bit is the
most significant bit of the first byte; a final partial byte is padded with
zero bits (only `codeword_hex` of the header code, N = 780, has padding). A
bit value of 0 is sent as +1 by BPSK; the vectors do not depend on this.

## `bitmap/<case>.json` (ADR 0005 bit mapping)

Generated by `uv run --package cairn-sim python -m cairn_sim.bitmap_vectors`
(`sim/cairn_sim/bitmap_vectors.py`); do not edit by hand. Each file follows
one frame through every stage of ADR 0005, from payload to tone values and,
for three cases, to time-domain samples. `sim/tests/test_bitmap_vectors.py`
regenerates them byte for byte and checks each stage against the rules,
written out independently of the simulator.

| Case | Width | MCS | Data blocks | Prefix | Mask (tones off) | Samples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `w24_mcs0` … `w24_mcs6` | 24 | 0–6 | 2 | 60 | none | `w24_mcs6` only |
| `w24_mcs0_long` | 24 | 0 | 1 | 120 | none | yes |
| `w24_mcs3_long_masked` | 24 | 3 | 2 | 120 | 0, 1, 2 | no |
| `w30_mcs1_masked` | 30 | 1 | 2 | 60 | 9, 25 (and 40, above the width, ignored) | no |
| `w56_mcs2`, `w56_mcs6` | 56 | 2, 6 | 2 | 60 | none | no |
| `w56_mcs4_long_masked` | 56 | 4 | 2 | 120 | 0, 1, 2, 37 (a pilot), 45, 46 | yes |

Frames wider than 24 tones carry the two-symbol width probe after the
header, with the probe width equal to the link width. There are no control
blocks here; `frame/` below has frames with them.

Inputs are pseudo-random bytes: SHA-256 of the ASCII string
`cairn-bitmap-vector:<case>:<item>:<counter>`, counter = 0, 1, …,
concatenated, with item `header` (16 bytes, the header's 128 bits before
its CRC) or `block<i>` (126 payload bytes of data block i). The header bits
are arbitrary and do not follow the SPEC 8.2 field layout; the bit mapping
does not depend on them.

Top-level fields:

- `format` (`cairn-bitmap-vectors`), `version`, `license`, `name`.
- `width` (W), `mcs`, `modulation`, `bits_per_point` (Q), `code_rate`,
  `coded_bits` (E of each data block), `interleaver_a` (a for E),
  `prefix_samples` (60 or 120).
- `usable_tones`: the 56-bit mask report as a list of usable tones, or
  `null` for no mask. `masked_tones`: the tones below the width it turns
  off.
- `control_blocks` (always 0), `data_blocks`, `total_symbols`.
- `segments`: in frame order, each with `kind` (`preamble`, `header`,
  `probe`, `data`), `first_symbol` (s, counted from the first preamble
  symbol), `symbols`, and either `tones` (preamble, probe) or
  `data_tones` and `pilot_tones` (lowest first) and `gain` (the scale on
  every active tone of the segment's symbols).
- `header`: `position` (0), `c_init`, `coded_bits` (288), `interleaver_a`,
  `message_hex`, then the stages below, plus `pad_bits`, `pad_hex` (the
  padding that completes the header's last symbol) and `points_iq`.
- `blocks`: one per data block, with `index`, `position` (1 + index),
  `c_init` (1 + position), `first_symbol_offset` and `first_tone_slot`
  (where the block's first point lands, relative to the first data symbol
  and as an index into the data tones), `payload_hex`, then the stages
  below.
- `data`: `pad_bits` and `pad_hex` (the padding after the last block),
  `point_scale` (1/√2, 1/√10, 1/√42 or 1/√170), and `points_iq`, the points of
  the whole data segment: blocks, then padding.
- `grid`: `re` and `im`, each `total_symbols` rows of W values: the tone
  values X[k] of every symbol, with gains applied, before the time-domain
  scale. Printed to 9 decimal places.
- `samples_scale` and `samples` (three cases only): the frame's
  time-domain samples at 48 kHz, symbol after symbol, each symbol being
  prefix plus useful part, x(n) = A·Re{Σ X[k]·exp(j2π(3.5 + k)n/480)} with
  A = `samples_scale` = √(2/W), and no clip-and-filter (the clipper is not
  bit-exact and its level depends on the rung, ADR 0003). Printed to 6
  decimal places. The scale is the simulator's (unit mean power for W
  unit-power tones); only the relative values are normative.

Per-block stages, as big-endian hex like the LDPC vectors:

- `info_hex`: payload (or header message) followed by its CRC-16.
- `rate_matched_hex`: the E bits after LDPC encoding and rate matching
  (identical to `rate_matched_hex` of the LDPC vectors for the same
  information bits).
- `scrambler_hex`: c(0 … E−1), the Gold sequence for this block's
  `c_init`.
- `scrambled_hex`: `rate_matched_hex` XOR `scrambler_hex`.
- `interleaved_hex`: the scrambled bits permuted, scrambled bit i at
  position (a·i) mod E.

`points_iq` lists the constellation points before scaling as odd integers,
I then Q for each point (I0, Q0, I1, Q1, …). Multiply by `point_scale` (1/√2 for
the header, always QPSK) and the segment `gain` to get the tone values in
`grid`. Point m of a segment
lies in symbol ⌊m/D⌋ of the segment, on `data_tones[m mod D]`.

## `header/<case>.json` (SPEC 8.2, B.1)

Generated by `uv run --package cairn-sim python -m cairn_sim.frame_vectors`
(`sim/cairn_sim/frame_vectors.py`, with `header/`, `control/`, `frame/` and
`link/`); do not edit by hand. `sim/tests/test_frame_vectors.py` regenerates
them byte for byte and packs every valid case from its fields with the SPEC
tables written out independently of the simulator.

Fields: `format` (`cairn-header-vectors`), `version`, `license`, `name`,
`description`, `valid`, then either `fields` (valid cases) or `error`
(`crc` or `fields`: a header a receiver must discard), and `message_hex`
(the 128 bits before the CRC) and `info_hex` (all 144 bits), big-endian hex
as above.

`fields` (ADR 0011 `header-field-layout`): `version`, `frame_type`, `flags`
(the 8-bit field's value), `more_fragments`, `session`, `probe`, `repeat`,
`first_fragment`, `block_map` (flag bits 2-7), `source` and `destination`,
`sequence`, `mcs`, `control_blocks` (0-3), `data_blocks` (0-128; sent as
count − 1 when flag bit 1 is set, as 0 otherwise) and `mask_id` (the 2-bit
field after the data block count; one reserved zero bit follows it). Flag
bits 0 and 1 are set exactly when the control and data block counts are
nonzero. An address is `{callsign, ssid, value_hex}` (the 40-bit field),
`{callsign, hash30, ssid, value_hex}` for a 7-10 character callsign sent as
code 63 and its hash (a receiver recovers only `hash30`), or
`{broadcast: true, value_hex: "ffffffffff"}`.

| Case | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `beacon` | BEACON to broadcast, one control and one data block, flag bit 6 (a complete message) |
| `ui_fragment_repeat` | UI first fragment (flag bits 6 and 2), repeat flag, MCS 3, 128 data blocks, frame counter 65535 |
| `ui_middle_fragment` | UI middle fragment (flag bit 2 only), frame counter 0 |
| `connect`, `connect_ack` | Setup frames: probe, B.3 and ID blocks, session flag on CONNECT-ACK, Mask ID 0 |
| `data`, `data_block_map`, `ack` | Session frames: three control blocks at MCS 6; a block map (flag bit 7) with Mask ID 3; an ACK frame with Mask ID 2 |
| `probe_long_callsign` | BEACON-request PROBE from `N0CALL/7`, a hashed callsign, with its frame counter |
| `extended_mcs_ignored` | Type 31 with MCS field 15 and no data blocks (valid: the field is ignored) |
| `reject_crc` | CRC failure |
| `reject_flag0_mismatch` | Flag bit 0 clear with two control blocks (SPEC 8.2: discard) |
| `reject_version` | Version 2 |
| `reject_address_code` | A reserved character code (39) in the source address |

Changes for ADRs 0008-0012: every case's `fields` gained `first_fragment`,
`block_map` and `mask_id`. Bits changed where the content did: `beacon`
and `ui_fragment_repeat` set flag bit 6; `connect_ack` carries two control
blocks (B.3 and ID, ADR 0010); `data` and `ack` carry Mask IDs 1 and 2,
`ack` the sender's next unsent sequence (17) and `probe_long_callsign` a
frame counter (4242). `ui_middle_fragment` and `data_block_map` are new.
`connect`, `extended_mcs_ignored` and the four rejected headers keep their
bits.

## `control/<case>.json` (Appendix B)

Same layout with `format` `cairn-control-vectors`, 240-bit `message_hex`
and 256-bit `info_hex`, and `error` `crc`, `fields` (malformed: discard the
block) or `unknown` (a reserved control type: skip the block and process
the rest of the frame). `fields` has `type` and, by type:

- 0 ACK (B.2, ADR 0011 `ack-control-block-layout`): `base_sequence` (the
  receiver's next in-order sequence, ADR 0008 `cumulative-ack`),
  `bitmap_hex` (the 128-bit field; bit i, of weight 2^i, set when the
  receiver holds block base + i, so bit 0 is clear), `requested_mcs`,
  `snr_q` (0.25 dB steps), `block_errors`, `tone_mask` (usable tones, or
  `null` when flag bit 0 is clear and the field is sent as zeros),
  `reprobe` (flag bit 2), `long_prefix` (flag bit 3), `distortion`,
  `adc_clipped`, `mask_id` (2 bits after `adc_clipped`; six reserved zero
  bits follow).
- 1 CONNECT / CONNECT-ACK (B.3): `session_id`, `capabilities`, `width_cap`,
  `requested_mcs`, `max_frame_time`, `tone_mask`, `dictionary_ids` (four
  slots, the first in the most significant bits), `more_dictionaries`
  (flag bit 0), `resume` (flag bit 1). A block whose width cap and maximum
  frame time are both 0 is the dictionary-only second block (ADR 0010
  `session-establishment-steps`); other values outside 23-55 and 5-40 are
  malformed.
- 3 CAL-REQUEST (B.5): `tone_freq`, `step_count`, `step_length`,
  `gap_length`, `start_level`, `step_size`.
- 4 CAL-REPORT (B.5): `amplitudes` and `distortion` (steps 1-8, step 1 in
  the most significant bits), `clipped` (step j at bit j − 1),
  `sweep_valid`.
- 5 ID (B.6): `callsign` (ten ASCII characters, space-padded), `ssid`,
  `grid` (`null` when absent; the field is then zeros).
- 6 Path (B.7): `entries` (0-2 addresses as above; unused entries are
  zeros), `hops`, `used` (flag bits 0 and 1), `via` (flag bit 2).
- 7 Block map (B.8, in `link/`): `base_sequence`, `bitmap_hex` (bit i set:
  block base + i is in the frame), `sequences` (the data blocks' sequences
  in frame order, derived from the bitmap).

A tone mask lists usable tones; tone k is bit k (weight 2^k) of the 56-bit
field. Cases: `ack` (cumulative, a mask with ID 1), `ack_mask_flags` (every
field at an extreme, Mask ID 3), `ack_refusal` (all zero, a refusal
DISCONNECT's block), `connect`, `connect_dictionaries` (the dictionary-only
second B.3), `connect_ack_resume`, `cal_request` (the B.5 defaults),
`cal_report`, `id_grid` (a 7-character callsign and a locator),
`id_no_grid`, `path`, and `reject_crc`, `reject_width_cap` (width cap 10),
`skip_reserved_type` (type 8).

Changes for ADRs 0008-0012: ACK `fields` gained `mask_id`. `ack` and
`ack_mask_flags` were rewritten as cumulative ACKs (bit 0 clear) with tone
masks and Mask IDs; `reject_crc` follows `ack`. `connect` sends requested
MCS 0 (ADR 0010 `connect-requested-mcs-zero`). `skip_reserved_type` uses
type 8, since type 7 is now the block map (ADR 0008
`reserved-control-types-after-block-map`). `ack_refusal` and
`connect_dictionaries` are new; the other cases keep their bits.

## `frame/<case>.json` (whole frames with real headers and control blocks)

Whole frames in the `bitmap/` format, built from SPEC 8.2 headers and
Appendix B control blocks. Additions to that format:

- `description`: one line on what the frame shows.
- `header.fields`: the header's fields as in `header/`.
- `clip_db`: the clip level the frame uses on air, the data rung's or
  7 dB without data blocks (SPEC 7.1; the vectors themselves are before
  clip-and-filter).
- `block_sequences` (frames with data blocks): the sequence each data block
  carries, in frame order: consecutive from the header's Sequence, or as
  the block map names them (ADR 0008).
- A `control` segment between the probe (if any) and the data: `data_tones`
  are tones 0-23 less pilots 3 and 20 and less masked tones, `pilot_tones`
  [3, 20], `gain` √(24 / (data tones + 2)) (ADR 0005), `symbols`
  ⌈512 · n / (2 · data tones)⌉.
- `controls`: one per control block, with `index`, `position` (1 + index),
  `c_init`, `first_symbol_offset` and `first_tone_slot` (relative to the
  first control symbol), `fields` as in `control/`, `message_hex`, and the
  per-block stages as above at E = 512 (interleaver a = 317).
- `control`: `pad_bits`, `pad_hex`, `point_scale` (1/√2) and `points_iq` of
  the control segment.
- Data blocks take positions 1 + n_control + index. A frame without data
  blocks has no `data` segment or section; `mcs`, `coded_bits` and the
  rest describe MCS 0 there and carry nothing.

`width` is the frame's width W: in a session frame the sender's session
width (ADR 0011 `session-width`), outside a session the width cap + 1 of
CONNECT, PROBE and the PROBE's SOUND reply. The probe, when present, covers
tones 0 … W − 1 (ADR 0011 `width-probe-extent`). The mask is the one the
header's Mask ID names; CONNECT, CONNECT-ACK and frames outside a session
are unmasked (ADR 0011 `mask-id`). Every frame keeps the control-block
order of its type (ADR 0010 `control-block-order`,
`session-establishment-steps`; ADR 0012 `probe-frame-contents`).

Payload bytes come from SHA-256 of `cairn-frame-vector:<case>:block<i>:<counter>`.

| Case | Type | Width | MCS | Control blocks | Data blocks | Prefix | Mask (tones off) | Samples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `connect_w30` | CONNECT | 30 (probe) | — | CONNECT, ID | 0 | 60 | none | no |
| `connect_ack_resume_probe` | CONNECT-ACK | 24 (probe) | — | CONNECT (resume), ID, ACK | 0 | 60 | none | no |
| `data_ack_w24_mcs2` | DATA | 24 | 2 | ACK | 2 | 60 | none | no |
| `data_ack_w30_mcs3_long_masked` | DATA | 30 (probe) | 3 | ACK | 3 | 120 | 1, 9, 25 (and 40, ignored), Mask ID 1 | no |
| `data_three_controls_w56_mcs4` | DATA | 56 (probe) | 4 | ACK, ID, CAL-REPORT | 2 | 60 | none | no |
| `ack` | ACK | 24 | — | ACK | 0 | 60 | none | yes |

Changes for ADRs 0008-0012 (every file also gained the fields above and
`header.fields` the new header fields): `connect_w30` sends requested MCS
0; `connect_ack_resume_probe` carries B.3, ID, then the resume ACK (ADR
0008 `session-resume-rules`, ADR 0010 `session-establishment-steps`); every
session ACK block is cumulative and carries the tone mask with its Mask ID
(ADR 0008 `cumulative-ack`, ADR 0011 `mask-in-every-session-ack`);
`data_ack_w30_mcs3_long_masked` names its mask by Mask ID 1; `ack` carries
the sender's next unsent sequence (ADR 0008 `block-sequence-rules`).
`probe_header_only` moved to `link/probe_beacon_request` (with its frame
counter in the Sequence, ADR 0010 `broadcast-frame-counter`).

## `link/<case>.json` (ADRs 0008-0012)

Generated with `header/` … `frame/` above; same formats. Payload bytes come
from SHA-256 of `cairn-link-vector:<case>:block<i>:<counter>`.

| Case | Format | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `block_map_retransmit` | control | Block map (B.8) of blocks 300, 302, 305-307 |
| `block_map_wrap` | control | Block map across the wrap: 65533, 65535, 0, 124 (bit 127) |
| `data_block_map_w24_mcs3` | frame | DATA, 24 tones, MCS 3: ACK block, block map, five non-consecutive blocks across the wrap (65533, 65535, 0, 2, 3), tones 5 and 17 masked under Mask ID 2 |
| `connect_ack_w30_id` | frame | New-session CONNECT-ACK: probe to W = 30, B.3, ID, dictionary-only B.3 |
| `probe_link_test_w40` | frame | Link-test PROBE: frame counter 4660, link-test B.3, probe to 40 tones (cap 39) |
| `sound_probe_reply_w32` | frame | The SOUND reply: Sequence 0, probe to its own 32 tones, one measurement ACK block (base, bitmap, errors 0; mask, Mask ID 0) |
| `probe_beacon_request` | frame | BEACON-request PROBE: no control blocks, no probe; with samples |
| `pdu_blocks` | PDU | PDUs into block payloads: filler, the one-byte rule, a PDU over three blocks, encoding 2 |
| `pdu_broadcast_fragments` | PDU | A 500-byte message as three UI frames, one PDU each |

PDU vectors (`format` `cairn-pdu-vectors`, ADR 0009) have:

- `pdu_blocks`: `crc32_check` (CRC-32/ISO-HDLC of ASCII `123456789`,
  `cbf43926`); `pdus`, each with `group`, `type`, `stream` (the whole
  Stream byte), `encoding`, `dictionary_id_hex` (`null` unless encoding 2),
  `length` (the Length field: every byte after it, CRC-32 included),
  `body_hex`, `crc32_hex`, `pdu_hex` (the PDU as sent: big-endian Length,
  Type, Stream, Encoding, Dictionary ID, body, big-endian CRC-32), and
  `block`, `offset` (where its Length field starts); `blocks_hex` (the
  126-byte payloads in order) and `block_info_hex` (each payload with its
  CRC-16, the data block's 1,024 information bits, ADR 0009
  `data-block-byte-order`). Each `group` is packed from a block boundary
  and its last block completed with zeros (filler: a zero Length where a
  PDU would start); a PDU that would leave one byte in its block is
  followed by one 0x00 byte. The encoding-2 PDU's body stands in for
  compressed bytes; its CRC-32 is over `decoded_hex`.
- `pdu_broadcast_fragments`: `message_hex`, `slice_bytes`, `reassembled`
  (the delivered PDU), and `frames`, each with `sequence` (the frame
  counter), `first_fragment` and `more_fragments` (header flag bits 6 and
  2), `header` (UI header fields as in `header/`) and `header_info_hex`,
  `pdu` (as above) and `blocks_hex`.

## `zstd/bodies.json` (SPEC 12.1-12.2, ADR 0014 `zstd-frame-format`)

Generated by `uv run --package cairn-sim python -m cairn_sim.zstd_vectors`
(`sim/cairn_sim/zstd_vectors.py`) through the dictionary evaluation's codec
(`dict_eval.Codec`, python-zstandard 0.25.0, libzstd 1.5.7, level 19); do not
edit by hand. `sim/tests/test_zstd_vectors.py` regenerates it byte for byte,
and `modem/link/tests/zstd_vectors.rs` checks the reference modem against it.
Format `cairn-zstd-vectors`:

- `dictionary`: the dictionary used for encoding 2, by `file`, `sha256` and
  `dictionary_id_hex`. It is `dictionaries/eval/eval-forms-64k.zdict`, an
  evaluation artifact rather than a released dictionary (see
  `dictionaries/REGISTRY.md`); the vectors change if it is replaced.
- `decoded_body_max`: 65,528, the largest body a raw PDU carries (65,535 -
  7), which the reference modem uses as its bound on a decoded body.
- `sent`: bodies a sender produces, for three fixed inputs (an ICS-213-style
  message, a short status line, 128 bytes of repeated SHA-256 output), each
  with encoding 1 and 2: `input_hex`, `body_hex` (one zstd frame: magic
  number, frame header descriptor 0x00, so no content size, no checksum, no
  in-frame dictionary ID; then the window descriptor), `header` (the
  descriptor's fields), and the whole PDU (`pdu_hex`: MESSAGE, stream 0,
  CRC-32 over the input). Byte equality is a property of the library and
  level, not a protocol requirement: the level is the sender's choice.
- `accept`: frames a receiver must decode although a sender would not write
  them: content size, checksum, and the library defaults with a dictionary
  (content size and in-frame dictionary ID, with and without the checksum).
- `reject`: bodies a receiver refuses, with `error`: `missing_magic` (the
  magic number cut off; a magicless-format frame), `trailing_bytes` (a byte,
  or a skippable frame, after the frame) and `too_large` (65,529 zeros, one
  over the bound, with and without a content size in the header).

Body sizes in bytes (the PDU adds 9 bytes, 13 with a Dictionary ID):

| Input | Raw body | Encoding 1 body | Encoding 2 body (64 kB) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ics213` | 278 | 219 | 129 |
| `status` | 84 | 89 | 64 |
| `random` | 128 | 50 | 50 |

## Field readings

Where the SPEC leaves an encoding open, the vectors follow the ADRs rather
than readings of their own:

- Bit numbering, one-bit-per-item fields, multi-bit sequences, absent and
  reserved fields, the header's MCS without data blocks, malformed
  addresses, SNR quantization and grid-locator padding: ADR 0007
  (`field-bit-order`, `absent-and-reserved-fields`,
  `header-mcs-without-data`, `malformed-addresses`,
  `snr-field-quantization`, `grid-locator-padding`).
- Header layout (flag bits 6 and 7, Mask ID) and ACK layout (Mask ID):
  ADR 0011 (`header-field-layout`, `ack-control-block-layout`).
- Sequence numbers, the cumulative ACK and the block map: ADR 0008
  (`block-sequence-rules`, `cumulative-ack`, `block-map-control-block`,
  `reserved-control-types-after-block-map`).
- PDU framing, filler, byte order, CRC-32, data-block byte order and
  broadcast fragments: ADR 0009.
- Control-block orders, the frame counter and the fixed Sequence values
  outside a session: ADR 0010 (`session-establishment-steps`,
  `control-block-order`, `broadcast-frame-counter`, `connect-refusal`).
- Width, probe extent and masks by ID: ADR 0011 (`session-width`,
  `width-probe-extent`, `mask-id`); PROBE contents and the SOUND reply:
  ADR 0012.

The second, dictionary-only B.3 block sends its width cap and maximum frame
time as 0, outside their ranges, so a B.3 block with both 0 is read as that
block and any other out-of-range value is malformed. This began as the
simulator's own reading (`sim/cairn_sim/fields.py`) and is now ADR 0010's
interpretation `dictionary-only-b3-marker`.
